
Business Process Description

The selected business process is Restaurant Order and Sales Management
The process begins when a customer selects food or drinks and places an order
The restaurant staff receives and records the order in the system
The order is then prepared and served to the customer
After that, the system calculates the total bill based on the ordered items and their prices
The customer makes a payment and the staff records the payment in the system finally
the system stores sales information and generates reports to help the restaurant manager monitor daily and monthly sales
The main purpose of this process is to improve order management ensure accurate bill calculation
maintain proper payment records and make sales tracking easier and more efficient
